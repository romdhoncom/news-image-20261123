# Monday 23 November 2026 in one image

A dense newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Monday
23 November 2026: the COP31 collapse in Antalya, Asian markets in freefall,
tankers burning in the Strait of Hormuz, Marc Marquez's eighth title, and the
Nepal prime minister's talks in New Delhi — plus a world map with 12 pins and
38 more stories from around the world.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) draws the page
with its own raster canvas and its own PNG encoder and writes
`/output/entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB). Two runs write byte-identical
files. Every story is a real event that happened or was first reported on
23 November 2026, or a weekend event the Monday paper reports.
